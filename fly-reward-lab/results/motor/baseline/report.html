Connectivity evidence
The graph provenance is recorded below. Anatomical connection counts alone do not determine synaptic strength, receptor effects, or neural dynamics.
Physiology assumptions
Firing rates, dopamine release and clearance, sensitivity, and tolerance are model assumptions. NPF is an abstract global proxy, without verified cell or receptor mapping. These are not measured drug responses.
Higher dopamine exposure or a larger reward proxy is a numerical model outcome. This report does not establish subjective pleasure or biological validation.
